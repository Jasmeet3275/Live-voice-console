The correction
A low-confidence field does not block confirmation. It starves everything downstream of it. If the service word is unresolved, the AI cannot score stylist capability, cannot compute duration, and therefore cannot produce slots at all. The rail has to render that as withheld, not as a guess the operator might trust.

Every intervention has the same seven parts
Where — the stage and field that stopped.
What — one plain sentence, no model jargon.
Evidence — the 2s clip, the decline code, the stale timestamp. Never “low confidence” alone.
Consequence — what is withheld downstream, named.
Choices — two or three resolutions, safest first, the AI’s pick marked.
Say next — the line the caller hears after you choose. It is a phone call; every resolution has a spoken consequence.
Reversibility — undo window, or an explicit warning that there is none.

Colour now carries three meanings only. Warm neutral is everything the system knows. Teal is the AI’s own voice and its recommendation. Amber is “a human must act”, at two weights. Red survives as one mark: the recording dot. Stale and lost data are struck neutral, not red — they are out of date, not dangerous.
A caller is on the line. Every hold shows dead-air elapsed, and the AI is given a stall line to buy the operator time. An intervention that takes 20 seconds to resolve is a bad intervention even if it resolves correctly.

1Zoca Front Desk — live call console, design pass
What I read in the current build
The screen has no spine. Everything lives in one centre column with a floating caller card beside it and a large empty left half. Nothing tells the operator where to look when the call is live.
Audio is decorative. 0:19 / 0:21 in the header is the only trace of the call. The assignment asks for audio state connected to the transcript.
Uncertainty is hidden, not surfaced. Every turn renders identically at full confidence. There is nowhere for “fade” misheard as “facial” to live, so the human-judgment moment never appears.
The booking is a receipt, not a decision. Slots, reasoning, and the edit affordances all collapse into one post-hoc summary table. The operator never sees why Marco at 6:30 was the recommendation.
Agent steps and speech compete. Bordered “Done” rows interleave with transcript bubbles at the same visual weight, so the thread reads as a log rather than a conversation.
Take Over has no gravity. A disabled footer button carries the assignment’s most important interaction. Handoff should be the most deliberate control on the screen.
The redesign: three fixed zones — who is calling, what is being said, what the AI wants to do — with one transport bar across the bottom that owns audio and handoff. Confidence is rendered inline in the transcript and propagates into the booking panel, so low-confidence words visibly block confirmation until a human resolves them.
The first-pass console lives in Zoca Front Desk Console v1.dc.html — lifted out of this file so two full 1320px consoles are not laid out at once. Its diagnosis above still applies; turn 2 supersedes its colour use and its right rail.
